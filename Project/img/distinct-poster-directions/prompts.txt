Create a SINGLE landscape 16:9 final exhibition poster design for WHAT REMAINS / 움직임의 잔상. No welcome UI, no comparison panels, no frame or wall mockup. This is a new, strongly art-directed visual proposal. Bright cold ground, NOT beige and NOT dark overall. The picture consists entirely of multiple visitor motion afterimages accumulated over time, not a pre-made landscape. No mountains, ocean, fabric, aurora or decorative light ribbons.
Human PRESENCE only: vague head/shoulder mass and one partial bent arm may be recognizable, but contours dissolve and overlap into abstraction. Absolutely NO dancers, ballet, choreographed group, anatomically clean posed people, identifiable faces, fingers, clothing, skin rendering, rainbow costumes, ethereal fairy illustration or watercolor painting. Most body detail must be obscured by optical blur and exposure. Work like photographed light/ink with deep cores and feathered chromatic bleeding, fine optical noise.
Reference images are MATERIAL AND DESIGN references, not objects to copy: never include the cat or reference's original text. Retain the text "WHAT REMAINS" and optional small "움직임의 잔상", plus at most a few tiny fragments like "COLLECTIVE MOTION" and "10 SEC / MANY TRACES". Important type must be >=9% inset from image edges, but artistic forms can bleed. Clean intentional editorial construction. Avoid default centered title above a line of colorful humans.
