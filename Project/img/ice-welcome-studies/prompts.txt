01-light-ice-white.png — 라이트 1 · 아이스 화이트
Design a single full-screen widescreen 16:9 flat digital WELCOME SCREEN for the same hands-free Korean media art exhibition as the reference. The reference is the already chosen FINAL poster. Extract its graphic identity only: cold silver-ice-blue atmosphere, very fine photographic grain, layered abstract motion forms with denser navy cores and selectively diffused cyan edges, a subtle suggestion of motion. Remove all existing English and Korean poster typography and recompose the artwork to make an exceptionally clean quiet text zone near the centre. Preserve the mood and only small hints of the fluid streaks; abstract the human details further so no face, head, arm, or other anatomy is legible. A faint thin arc may appear only as part of the edge artwork where specified, never run through the instructions. No warm beige, hanji, textile, 3D cloth, repeated round blobs or neon.
This is a polished exhibit interface that has no touch targets. Use consistent, crisp contemporary Korean sans-serif like Pretendard, careful tracking and line spacing, enough contrast at a distance. Avoid the huge Latin serif and fashion-poster typography of the final poster. Only these exact texts, each written once, in this order:
Top centered small above the central copy: “참여형 미디어 아트”
Main large headline: “움직임의 잔상” with a small optional accent-colored period.
Brief context line: “10초 동안 자유롭게 움직여 보세요.”
Main gesture cue on its own line, semibold and immediately obvious: “한 손을 머리 위로 들어 주세요”
Smaller supporting line: “잠시 유지하면 시작됩니다”
A restrained horizontal set of three steps right below the cue, ALL near the central copy: “손을 들어 시작”  “10초의 움직임”  “하나의 풍경”. Space or thin short rules between them.
Optional small “0000” at lower right, well inset, no extra label. Keep all essential instructions above y=72% and at least 12% from the sides. Keep a wide clear gap between the top label and the title, and a smaller gap between related lines. Text must never touch the frame. Text is ordinary matte solid letters, with no outlines or glow. A hairline rule near top is optional. No English slogans, exhibit credits, pictograms, icons, webcam video, mouse, touch, button, cards, or fake website navigation. Full-bleed single image, no mockup, device, collage, before/after panel, comparison, or annotations.
Use a pearl white and frost-blue field with ink-navy text, closest companion to the selected final poster. Keep a group of optical afterimage forms mainly lower right and a delicate translucent fragment in the far left. Preserve an open white area around the text. Centered editorial masthead at y=9%, heading at y=30%, gesture cue around y=48%, supporting line around y=55%, experience steps at y=64%. A restrained blue period in the title. Refined, bright and immediate, familiar website layout.
